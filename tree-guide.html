Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be near the tree to harvest it? ＋
No — once planted, trees grow on their own. You can harvest from anywhere in the game as long as the tree has reached the required GP level.
How long does a tree take to grow to 500,000 GP? ＋
Growth time varies based on the tree type and whether you apply fertilizer. Using fertilizer (💊 from the shop) can significantly reduce the time. Without it, trees grow steadily over several days.
Can I plant trees in multiple locations? ＋
Yes! You can move your character to different spots on the map and plant batches of seedlings in multiple locations. Buy more seedlings and repeat the process.
What is GP and how do I get it to buy seedlings? ＋
GP (Game Points) is the in-game currency. You earn GP by playing Hunt & Hide, finding treasure boxes, completing daily quests, and other game activities. You can also top up GP via the shop using Telegram Stars.
Can I harvest a tree before it hits 500,000 GP? ＋
Yes — mid-growth harvests are possible. You will receive the current GP value of the tree at the time of harvest. Waiting until 500,000 GP maximizes your TonCoin return.
